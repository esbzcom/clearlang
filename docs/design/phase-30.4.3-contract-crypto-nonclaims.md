# Phase 30.4.3 - Contract Crypto Non-Claims

Date: 2026-08-09
Status: Locked
Owner: crypto-and-proof-owner

ClearLang makes no contract-level claim about cryptographic hardness, collision resistance,
preimage resistance, unforgeability, randomness quality, timing behavior, cache behavior, power
analysis, or any other side-channel property. Such properties remain outside source proofs and
release evidence unless a future primitive-specific design explicitly defines and independently
validates them.

Tooling integrity checks remain scoped to their artifacts and do not expand these claims.
